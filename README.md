# Mamello Projects
> Business website for a construction, welding, and general supply company serving the Vaal Triangle, South Africa.
**Live site:** (https://www.mamelloprojects.co.za)
---
## What It Is
A single-page marketing website for **Mamello Projects**, a local business that offers:
- **Construction** — new builds, renovations, roofing, paving, tiling, painting
- **Welding & Steel** — gates, carports, sheds, palisades, fencing, aluminium doors & windows, motor gate installations
- **General Supply** — gas refill & exchange, charcoal, wooden poles, tree felling, rubble removal
The site includes a project gallery, service breakdowns, customer testimonials, and click-to-call / WhatsApp contact buttons.
